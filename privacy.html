Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Mac Sweeper does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What Mac Sweeper does on your Mac

When you drop an app onto Mac Sweeper, it reads the names and sizes of files inside the Library folder you chose, to find files that belong to that app. When you click Move to Trash, it moves the files you see in the list to the Trash. It does not read the contents of your documents, and it does not send file names or any other information off your Mac.

Information we collect

None. Mac Sweeper has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. It does not connect to the internet.

Third parties

Mac Sweeper does not share information with anyone because it does not collect any. Apple processes downloads from the Mac App Store, and any purchases, under Apple's own policies. Apple does not give us your personal information through that process.

Children

Mac Sweeper is a general utility and is not aimed at children. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
