Get Mac Sweeper on the Mac App Store

How it works

  1. Open Mac Sweeper and click Allow Access to Library…. Choose the Library folder inside your home folder. macOS asks again each time you open the app.
  2. Drop an app onto the window. Mac Sweeper lists the app and every leftover file it finds.
  3. Check the list, then click Move to Trash.

What to expect

Mac Sweeper runs inside the Mac App Store sandbox, so it can only look where you allow it. That keeps your Mac safe. It also means a few things are out of reach: items owned by macOS itself, such as apps installed from the App Store, can't be removed by Mac Sweeper. Remove those in Finder.

Everything Mac Sweeper removes goes to the Trash first, so you can put it back.

Private by design

Mac Sweeper has no accounts, no analytics and no internet connection. The list of files it finds never leaves your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
